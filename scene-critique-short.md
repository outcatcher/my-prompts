Read the text and warn me if:
- sentences feel long or clause-heavy unintentionally
- verbs are weak or passive
- description slows pacing for no reason
- prose becomes dense or poetic in ways that break realism
- emotions feel over-explained
- character behavior seems unrealistic for someone of given age in given setting (by default 20-30 year old adult in 2025)

Also:
If a sentence feels tense, clipped, or intense on purpose to match mood (“mood burst”), treat it as intentional and not a problem.
